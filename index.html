Water tank monitoring

A sensor on the tank lid measures the water level and sends it by radio to a receiver in your house or shed. You see how full each tank is, how much you use a day, and roughly when it will run out, before it does.

From problem to installed system

Got a job that eats your time or keeps going wrong? Here's how we turn it into a machine that does it for you.

  1. Look at the problem

    We come out, see the site and work out what's actually going wrong and what it costs you.

  2. Build a prototype

    We design the sensors, electronics and mechanics, then test a working unit on the bench.

  3. Install and trial

    We fit it on site and run it alongside your current routine until it proves itself.

  4. Support it

    We keep looking after the system: firmware updates, spare parts and a person to call.
