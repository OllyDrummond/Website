Completed

Tank monitoring & pump control

On rural properties, tanks are often far from the house and checked by climbing a ladder, or not at all until the taps stop. A stainless pressure probe on the tank floor measures the depth. A solar-powered sensor unit sends it by LoRa radio to a receiver in the house, which uploads it over Wi-Fi so levels, usage and pump control are on your phone or computer.
