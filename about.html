About Kinetiq

Engineers who'd rather fix the problem than sell you a gadget.

Kinetiq is a New Zealand mechatronics business. We design and build practical machines, sensors and control systems for farms and rural properties.

Our story

It started with an empty tank

Rural life runs on systems most people never think about: water, pumps, filters, wash-downs. When they fail, you find out the hard way.

Our first project was a simple question: why do you have to climb a ladder to find out how much water is in the tank? The answer became the Kinetiq tank monitor, a sensor and receiver that does the checking for you.

Since then we've been turning other everyday farm jobs into machines that run themselves. Filter changes and acid dosing are next on the bench. Each system is designed in New Zealand for New Zealand conditions: long distances, patchy coverage, mud, weather and all.

How we do things

Built for the paddock

Enclosures that shrug off weather, radios that don't need cell coverage, batteries that last years. If it can't survive a farm, it doesn't leave the workshop.

Honest engineering

We'll tell you if a machine isn't the answer. When it is, we explain what it does, what it costs and what it needs from you, in plain language.

We stay on call

The people who designed your system are the people who service it. Spares, firmware updates and fault-finding all come straight from us.
