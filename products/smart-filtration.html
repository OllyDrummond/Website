Products / Smart filtration
Concept

Change filters when they need it, not when the calendar says.

Our smart filtration unit measures water pressure before and after each filter. As a cartridge clogs, the pressure difference rises, and the controller tells you which one to change and roughly when.

How it works

Pressure across each stage

Sensors either side of every housing show exactly which filter is clogging, whether it's sediment, carbon or UV.

Change reminders that mean something

You get an alert when a cartridge is close to the end of its life, based on real flow, so you don't throw away good filters or run with blocked ones.

Flow and usage logging

See how much water has gone through the system each day. Handy for household rain water, bores and small water schemes.

UV lamp monitoring

If a UV steriliser lamp fails or reaches its rated hours, you know straight away instead of finding out after a water test.

Automatic flush

Optional motorised valve back-flushes the sediment stage on a schedule or when pressure climbs.

Works with your existing housings

Designed to retrofit standard 10" and 20" filter housings, so you keep the cartridges you already buy.
