Projects / Smart filtration

Mock-up

Change filters when they need it, not when the calendar says.

Our smart filtration concept measures water pressure before and after each filter. As a cartridge clogs, the pressure difference rises, and the controller tells you which one to change and roughly when.

Register interest How it would work

This product is a design mock-up. The render shows how it could look, and the details below will change as we design and test it.

How it would work

The idea

Pressure across each stage

Sensors either side of every housing show exactly which filter is clogging: sediment, carbon or UV.

Change reminders that mean something

Alerts based on real flow, so you don't bin good filters or run with blocked ones.

Flow and usage logging

See how much water has gone through each day. Useful for rainwater, bores and small schemes.

UV lamp monitoring

Know straight away if a UV lamp fails or reaches its rated hours, not after a water test.

Automatic flush

An optional motorised valve back-flushes the sediment stage when pressure climbs.

Fits your existing housings

Designed to retrofit standard 10" and 20" housings, so you keep your usual cartridges.
