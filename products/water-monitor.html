About the Tank Monitor

Know how much water is in every tank on your property, without leaving the house.

Try the app in the live demo

A stainless pressure probe sits on the tank floor and measures the weight of the water above it, which gives the depth to the centimetre. Foam, condensation and lids don't affect the reading.

The tank unit on the lid reads the probe every 15 minutes and sends the level by LoRa long-range radio to a receiver in your house, up to 5 km away, with no mobile coverage needed. A small solar panel keeps its battery charged, so there are no wires to run.

The receiver shows every tank on its screen and uploads readings over your Wi-Fi to the Kinetiq app and website. There you can see each tank's level, daily use and days remaining, and get an alert before a tank runs low.

Add a pump controller and the system fills your tanks too: by hand from your phone, automatically by level, or on a schedule, with dry-run, overfill and run-time cut-outs.

Designed and built in New Zealand for dairy farms, sheep and beef, lifestyle blocks and rural homes.

Features

  • Reads from the tank floor

    A submersible pressure probe measures the weight of water above it. Foam, condensation and lids don't affect the reading.

  • Solar powered

    A small solar panel keeps the battery charged, so there are no wires to run and no batteries to swap.

  • Long-range radio

    LoRa radio reaches across paddocks and hills, with no mobile coverage or Wi-Fi needed at the tank.

  • Low-water alerts

    Set a level for each tank. When it drops below, you get an alert before the taps stop.

  • Pump control

    Fill the tank by hand, by level or on a schedule, with dry-run, overfill and run-time cut-outs.

  • Many tanks, one receiver

    House, stock and fire-fighting tanks all show in one place.
