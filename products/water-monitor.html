Three parts, one system

A tank unit on each tank, a receiver in the house and, where a pump fills the tank, a pump controller. All three talk over LoRa radio.

  • Tank unit

    Sits on the tank lid. A solar panel on top charges its battery, and the stainless probe on the tank floor measures the water depth.

    On the tank

  • Receiver

    Lives in the house. Its screen shows each tank's level and use, and it uploads readings over your Wi-Fi to the app and website.

    In the house

  • Pump controller

    Wired to the fill pump. It switches the pump by hand, by tank level or on a schedule, and cuts it out on dry-run, overfill or run time.

    At the pump

How it works

From the bottom of the tank to your phone

  1. Pressure probe

    A stainless probe sits on the tank floor and measures the weight of water above it, which gives the depth.

    In the tank

  2. Sensor unit

    Reads the probe on a schedule. A small solar panel keeps its battery topped up, so there are no wires to run.

    On the tank

  3. LoRa radio link

    Sends each reading by long-range radio to the house, across paddocks and hills, with no mobile coverage needed.

    Up to 5 km (target)

  4. Receiver

    Sits in the house or shed, shows every tank on its screen and uploads readings over your Wi-Fi.

    In the house

  5. App & website

    Levels, usage, alerts and pump control from your phone or computer, wherever you are.

    Anywhere

Features

Simple on the outside, clever on the inside

Reads from the tank floor

A submersible pressure probe measures the weight of water above it. Foam, condensation and lids don't affect the reading.

Solar powered

A small solar panel keeps the battery charged, so there are no wires to run and no batteries to swap.

Long-range radio

LoRa radio reaches across paddocks and hills with no mobile coverage or Wi-Fi needed at the tank.

Low-water alerts

Set a level for each tank. When it drops below, you get an alert before the taps stop.

Pump control

Fill the tank by hand, by level or on a schedule, with dry-run, overfill and run-time cut-outs.

Many tanks, one receiver

House, stock and fire-fighting tanks all show in one place.

Inside the unit

Built to sit on a tank for years

A sealed enclosure holds the controller board, LoRa radio and rechargeable battery, with the solar panel fixed on the lid.
