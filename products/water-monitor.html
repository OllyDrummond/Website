How it works

Five parts, one clear reading

  1. Pressure probe

    A stainless probe sits on the tank floor and measures the weight of water above it, which gives the depth.

    In the tank

  2. Sensor unit

    Reads the probe on a schedule. A small solar panel keeps its battery topped up, so there are no wires to run.

    On the tank

  3. LoRa radio link

    Sends each reading by long-range radio to the house, across paddocks and hills, with no mobile coverage needed.

    Up to 5 km (target)

  4. Receiver

    Sits in the house or shed, shows every tank on its screen and uploads readings over your Wi-Fi.

    In the house

  5. App & website

    Levels, usage, alerts and pump control from your phone or computer, wherever you are.

    Anywhere

Inside the unit

Built to sit on a tank for years

Everything lives in a sealed enclosure: the controller board, LoRa radio and a rechargeable battery kept topped up by the solar panel on top.

  • Pressure probe measures from the tank floor, so condensation, foam and lids don't affect it
  • No mains power or mobile signal needed at the tank
  • Fits concrete, steel and plastic tanks through an inlet or hatch

Features

What you get

Live level & litres

Percent full and litres for every tank, updated through the day.

Usage history

Daily water use and level trends, so you can spot changes in demand.

Low-water alerts

A text or app alert when a tank drops below the level you set.

Leak detection

A sudden drop overnight is flagged as a possible leak or burst pipe.

Pump control

Fill the tank by hand, by level, or on a schedule, with safety cut-outs.

Many tanks, one receiver

House, stock and fire-fighting tanks all show in one place.

The receiver
