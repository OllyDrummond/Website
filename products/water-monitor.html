From the tank floor to your phone.

On the tank.

The solar unit sits on the lid. Its stainless probe rests on the tank floor and measures the depth.

In the house.

The receiver shows every tank on its screen and sends readings over your Wi-Fi to the app.

At the pump.

Add a pump controller to fill tanks by hand, by level or on a schedule, with automatic safety cut-outs.

Fits through the lid.

The probe drops to the tank floor on its cable. Foam and condensation don't affect the reading.

Built to last outdoors.

A sealed case holds the circuit board, long-range radio and rechargeable battery. Designed and built in New Zealand.
