How it works

Simple on the outside, clever on the inside

Measures from the lid

An ultrasonic sensor measures the distance down to the water. Nothing goes in the tank, and it fits any tank with a lid or hatch.

Long-range radio

LoRa radio reaches across paddocks and hills with no cell coverage or Wi-Fi needed at the tank.

Years on a battery

The sensor wakes, reads, sends and sleeps. A small solar panel is available for very remote tanks.

Low-water alerts

Set a level for each tank. When it drops below, you get an alert before the taps stop.

Leak detection

A sudden overnight drop usually means a leak or burst pipe. The system flags it.

Many tanks, one screen

One receiver handles many sensors, so house, stock and fire-fighting tanks all show in one place.

The receiver

Readings on the bench, alerts on your phone

The receiver sits in the house or shed. It shows every tank on its screen and passes readings to the client portal over Wi-Fi.
