How it works

From the tank floor to your phone

  1. Probe on the tank floor

    A stainless pressure probe measures the weight of the water above it, which gives the depth. Foam, condensation and lids don't affect it.

  2. Tank unit on the lid

    Reads the probe every 15 minutes, faster while the pump runs. A small solar panel keeps its battery charged.

  3. Radio to the house

    Sends each reading by LoRa long-range radio, up to 5 km, with no mobile coverage needed at the tank.

  4. Receiver in the house

    Shows every tank on its screen and uploads readings over your Wi-Fi.

  5. App on your phone

    Levels, daily use, days remaining, low-water alerts and pump control, wherever you are.

    Questions

    Good to know

    Can't find your answer? Talk to an engineer

    Do I need mobile coverage at the tank?

    No. The tank unit talks to the receiver in your house by LoRa long-range radio, up to 5 km line of sight. The receiver needs your home Wi-Fi to send readings to the app.

    How is the tank unit powered?

    A small solar panel on its lid charges a rechargeable battery, so there are no wires to run to the tank.

    Does foam or condensation affect the reading?

    No. The probe sits on the tank floor and measures the weight of the water above it, rather than looking down at the surface.

    Can I monitor more than one tank?

    Yes. Add a tank unit for each tank. They all report to the same receiver, and you see them together in the app.

    Can it control my pump?

    Yes, with a pump controller wired to the fill pump. You can run it by hand from your phone, automatically by tank level, or on a schedule, with dry-run, overfill and run-time cut-outs.

    What about delivery and warranty?

    Kits ship from New Zealand, paired and ready for your site. Delivery costs and warranty terms are being finalised for launch.
