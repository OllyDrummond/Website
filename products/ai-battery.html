Projects / AI battery

Mock-up

Stored power that thinks ahead.

Our AI battery concept stores energy from solar or the grid and learns how your property uses it. It plans when to charge and when to draw down, so you rely on the grid less when power costs the most.

Register interest How it would work

This product is a design mock-up. The render shows how it could look, and the details below will change as we design and test it.

How it would work

The idea

Learns your daily pattern

It watches when the house, sheds and pumps draw power, and builds a picture of a normal day.

Plans around your solar

Stores midday solar instead of exporting it cheaply, ready for the evening and early-morning peaks.

Charges when power is cheap

Follows your tariff times, topping up off-peak and drawing down when grid power costs the most.

Backup for the essentials

Keeps the things that matter running in an outage, such as water pumps, fridges and the router.

One app for everything

Sits in the same app and portal as your tank monitor, so storage, water and pumps are in one place.

Slim, sealed enclosure

A quiet brushed-metal case for a wall or floor, with a single status light instead of a busy display.
