Specifications

A tank unit for each tank, one receiver for the house, and a pump controller if you choose pump control.

No more driving out to the back paddock to check the tank.

A probe on the tank floor measures the depth. A solar unit on the lid sends it by long-range radio to a receiver in the house, which puts the level, daily use and days remaining on your phone.
