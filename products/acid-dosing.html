Products / Automated acid dosing
Concept

The right acid dose in every dairy wash, without anyone pouring it.

Plant wash in the dairy shed often means someone measuring acid by hand, twice a day. Our dosing system draws straight from the drum, doses the right amount for each wash and keeps a record you can show your milk company.

How it works

Measured to the millilitre

A peristaltic dosing pump delivers a set volume for each wash cycle, so it's never too weak to clean or too strong for the rubberware.

No hand-handling

Acid goes straight from the drum to the wash line. Less splashing, fewer burns and no measuring jug in the shed.

Triggered by the wash

The controller senses when a wash starts and doses at the right point in the cycle automatically.

Wash records

Every wash is logged with time, dose and temperature, ready for audits or for your milk company.

Low-drum warning

A level sensor in the drum warns you before it runs out, so a wash never runs without acid.

Works with alkali too

The same unit can run a second pump for detergent or alkali on alternate washes.
